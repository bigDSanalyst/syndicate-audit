# Trace-distance characterization of recurrent pairwise synchronization in cavity-coupled superconducting qubits

## Abstract

> We theoretically investigate recurrent pairwise synchronization in three super?conducting qubits
> coupled to a common single-mode microwave cavity. The qubits interact indirectly through the
> cavity field and are driven by a coher?ent microwave field. We obtain the coherent dynamics in
> the dressed-state basis and quantify pairwise synchronization by the trace distance between
> reduced single-qubit density matrices. The trace-distance dynamics exhibit oscillatory wave-
> packet structures, with low-trace-distance regions identifying finite syn?chronization windows.
> An envelope-threshold procedure is introduced to extract the representative duration of these
> windows. By varying the middle-qubit coupling strength and the relative coupling ratio of the
> outer qubits, we con?struct a parameter-dependent map of the synchronization-window duration.
> The duration decreases rapidly with increasing relative outer-qubit coupling and becomes weakly
> dependent on the middle-qubit coupling in the stronger-coupling regime. These results provide a
> reduced-state-based characterization of coherent collective dynamics in multiqubit circuit-QED
> systems.
