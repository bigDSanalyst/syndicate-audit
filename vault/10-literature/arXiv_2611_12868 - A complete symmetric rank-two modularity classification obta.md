# A complete symmetric rank-two modularity classification, obtained through a sharp uniform degree bound

## Abstract

> We classify modular Nahm sums with a rational symmetric positive-definite two-by-two matrix and
> rational shifts. Up to coordinate exchange, every modular datum belongs to three explicit
> rational-point families, is a product of two modular rank-one sums, or is one of thirty-four
> exceptional triples on twelve matrices. The list consists of Zagier's rank-two examples, the
> Vlasenko--Zwegers additions and split products; no further data occur. The proof first bounds
> the joint degree of the distinguished Nahm point by four, using an initial degree bound of
> twenty-four and exact local exclusions at the prime 7. Explicit height and exponent bounds then
> give exhaustive arithmetic classifications in degrees one through four. Exact polynomial
> elimination determines all rational shifts, and published identities of q-series establish
> modularity of the survivors. The degree bound is sharp. The finite steps are accompanied by
> exact certificates, verification programs and independently implemented checks. Computational
> companion: https://github.com/johnngy44/math/releases
