# ExoVetter and a TESS Exoplanet Transit Search of Kepler Stars as Ground Truth

## Abstract

> We introduce a Python package called exoVetter that produces vetting metrics and visualizations
> to evaluate transiting exoplanet signals found in high-cadence time-series photometry. exoVetter
> is open source, modular, and independent of the mission data so that it can be easily expanded
> and used as part of different transit search pipelines tuned to different science cases and
> missions. We demonstrate its use in a pipeline as a way to distinguish real events found in a
> box-least squares exoplanet transit search of 324,530 light curves from the Transiting Exoplanet
> Survey Satellite mission; we limit our demonstration to stars also observed by the more
> sensitive Kepler telescope as a way to establish ground truth for the events found in the light
> curves. Using this ground truth, we provide baseline thresholds for several flux-level vetters
> and one centroid-offset vetter when using exoVetter metrics on TESS data. Our demonstration also
> indicates that it is possible to create a planet catalog from the light curves of dim targets
> (>13.5Tmag) that has a similar fraction of real events to that of the bright targets (<13.5Tmag)
> for short period events identified in the TESS-Gaia light curves.
