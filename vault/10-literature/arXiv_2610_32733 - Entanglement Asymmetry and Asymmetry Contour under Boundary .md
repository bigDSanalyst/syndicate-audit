# Entanglement Asymmetry and Asymmetry Contour under Boundary Symmetry Breaking

## Abstract

> We investigate how symmetry-breaking boundary magnetic fields affect magnetization and Rényi-2
> entanglement asymmetry (EA) in the transverse-field Ising model. For the ground state,
> magnetization exhibits qualitatively distinct behaviors in the ordered and disordered phases. EA
> also exhibits phase-dependent behavior, which is well captured by the lower and upper bounds
> derived in this work. In the critical regime, both magnetization and EA exhibit behavior
> qualitatively consistent with the field-theoretical predictions for a related setup. We also
> study boundary-quench dynamics and find that magnetization propagates from the boundary into the
> bulk with a velocity consistent with the maximum quasiparticle velocity. In contrast, because EA
> is a subsystem-integrated quantity, its spatial structure is not directly resolved. We therefore
> introduce the asymmetry contour, a contour-like finite-difference quantity that provides a
> spatially resolved characterization of the symmetry-breaking information encoded in the reduced
> density matrix. The resulting contour is complementary to the local magnetization, retaining
> sensitivity to nonlocal many-body correlations while its dynamics also reflects the underlying
> quasiparticle propagation. These results show how EA and its contour can provide complementary
> information-theoretic characterizations of symmetry breaking and its spatial structure.
