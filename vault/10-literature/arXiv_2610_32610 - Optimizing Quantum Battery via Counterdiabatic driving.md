# Optimizing Quantum Battery via Counterdiabatic driving

## Abstract

> We analyze the energy charging of a quantum battery within an open quantum framework, where the
> interaction between the battery element and an external power source is mediated by an ancilla
> system acting as a controllable switch. Specifically, we investigate the charging and
> discharging dynamics of a cavity-based quantum battery coupled to a driven qubit functioning as
> the charger. To ensure lossless power transfer, we utilize an adiabatic evolution protocol.
> Furthermore, we implement counterdiabatic driving, achieved by applying an auxiliary control
> field to the original Hamiltonian, to suppress undesired transitions and realize fast adiabatic
> dynamics. This scheme significantly accelerates the charging process. We demonstrate that the
> charging efficiency under adiabatic evolution is enhanced and exhibits stable, non-oscillatory
> behavior, and we determine the optimal duration for the operation. Finally, we show that
> counterdiabatic driving minimizes the path length between the initial and final states, inducing
> the shortest possible evolution distance.
