# Coarse Embeddings Beyond Doubling: Persistence Diagrams and Multiparameter Modules

## Abstract

> We study coarse embeddings into Hilbert space and the asymptotic dimension of spaces of
> persistence diagrams and multiparameter persistence modules. We replace doubling, the usual
> hypothesis for landmark embeddings, by admissible tent covers of bounded multiplicity at every
> scale: stacking them across dyadic scales gives an explicit $1$-Lipschitz coarse embedding, with
> lower gauge $c\sqrt{τt}$, or nearly linear for slowly decaying weights, and the same covers
> bound the asymptotic dimension by the multiplicity. The spaces of persistence diagrams are not
> doubling but carry such covers, and for diagrams with $n$ points our covers improve the constant
> of the known landmark embedding by a factor exponential in $n$. Slice by slice, the construction
> embeds multiparameter modules with boundedly many bars per slice under the matching distance on
> any finite net of slices, a class that contains the modules with at most $n$ generators.
> Bounding bars or persistent Betti numbers instead of generators fails: two-parameter modules
> with one bar on every slice have infinite asymptotic dimension, and neither they nor the modules
> of bounded support and bounded rank admit a coarse embedding into Hilbert space, under the
> matching or the interleaving distance.
