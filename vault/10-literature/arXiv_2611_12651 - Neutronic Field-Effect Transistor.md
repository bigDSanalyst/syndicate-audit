# Neutronic Field-Effect Transistor

## Abstract

> Field-effect electronic devices are in widespread use in communication, computing, data storage,
> and sensing applications. We demonstrate a neutronic field-effect transistor (NFET) that is
> analogous to the Datta-Das electron spin transistor and atomtronic devices. The NFET is based on
> a perfect-crystal Mach-Zehnder neutron interferometer with phase shifts applied to the spin and
> path degrees of freedom.
