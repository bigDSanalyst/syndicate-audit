# Fermion Scattering on Quantum Computers

## Abstract

> Resonant scattering links particle interactions to the formation and decay of unstable
> excitations. We develop a hybrid quantum-computing framework to study these dynamics through
> fermion--antifermion collisions. Our formulation of $(1+1)$-dimensional Yukawa theory encodes
> fermions in qubits and the scalar field in qumodes. We identify the operations needed for a
> photonic implementation and benchmark the dynamics with tensor-network simulations. Near
> resonance, we resolve the build-up of a localised scalar excitation and the subsequent partial
> return of energy to the fermions. Enhanced entanglement accompanies this exchange. Time-
> dependent correlator measurements reproduce the analytically predicted decay rate to a few
> percent precision, with energy scans exhibiting a resonance peak consistent with packet-averaged
> Breit--Wigner behaviour. These results establish a concrete scattering benchmark for hybrid
> quantum computers and demonstrate how local real-time observables can probe particle-resonance
> dynamics.
