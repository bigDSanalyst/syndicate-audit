# Spin Shuttling Through Quantum-Dot Based Chiral Molecular Bridges: Gauge Constraints, Holonomy, and Quantum Gates

## Abstract

> Chiral molecular bridges between silicon quantum dots can provide novel two-qubit entangling
> resources via the chirality-induced spin-selectivity (CISS) effect. We evaluate this using
> Makhlin invariants. Modeling a phase-coherent bridge as an $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-valued hopping
> between two spins, a Schrieffer-Wolff reduction yields a twisted exchange interaction $J
> \mathbf{S}_1 \cdot \mathbf{R} \mathbf{S}_2$, whose Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya and symmetric
> anisotropic parts are fixed by a single bridge angle $α$. Using the Kaplan-Shekhtman-Entin-
> Wohlman-Aharony (KSEA) gauge argument, we prove this interaction is locally equivalent to
> isotropic exchange; thus, a single chiral bond does not constitute a new entangling class.
> Breaking this gauge symmetry reveals genuine chiral utility: chiral exchange converts a uniform
> transverse field into a synthetic gradient, bringing the two-qubit propagator within
> $7\times10^{-4}$ of the CNOT class in invariant space 4.7 times faster than an equivalent
> orthogonal gradient. Shuttling a spin through bridges with non-collinear axes implements a non-
> Abelian $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ holonomy, yielding rotationally non-reciprocal transport and closed-
> loop geometric gates that suppress dynamical-phase noise to second order. To address the
> unsettled microscopic origin of CISS, we introduce a unified transport channel combining
> coherent rotation, a direction-reversing spin filter, and pointer-basis dephasing. This model
> obeys an exact $\tanh$ composition law for pumped polarization and a closed-form transverse
> decay law, yielding four observables that experimentally over-determine the bridge's coherent
> fraction $F_c$. Finally, we quantify silicon-specific hardware constraints, establishing the
> Landau-Zener valley-adiabaticity window ($E_v \geq 2.1\sqrt{\hbar v}$) and charge-noise limit
> ($σ_J/J \leq 3\%$) required for $99\%$ gate fidelity.
