# Universal semiclassical drift velocity of magnetic edge channels

## Abstract

> Magnetic edge channels, arising at the edge of a two-dimensional electron gas in a magnetic
> field, are topologically protected and propagate unidirectionally along the edge. Their group
> velocities at the Fermi level, being averaged harmonically, result in drift velocity, which is
> related to the edge density of states, quantum capacitance, and the velocity of edge
> magnetoplasmons. Using semiclassical quantization of electron skipping orbits, we show that the
> drift velocity equals 1/2 of the electron Fermi velocity in the limit of a large number of edge
> channels crossing the Fermi level. This result, being universal for a hard edge and for
> isotropic dispersion, is checked numerically for massive electrons and for massless electrons in
> graphene. The drift velocity at smooth electrostatically defined edges lacks such universality.
> In the case of anisotropic dispersion, the semiclassical drift velocity at the hard edge reduces
> to a coordinate-velocity correlation along the cyclotron orbit.
