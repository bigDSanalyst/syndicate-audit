# Effective Time Advance of Quantum Clocks under Postselection

## Abstract

> In the familiar twin paradox, the traveling twin ages less than the one who stays behind as a
> consequence of relativistic time dilation. Here, we show that quantum postselection can
> effectively reverse this outcome. Using an analytically solvable quantum clock model, we
> demonstrate that postselection of the center-of-mass motion produces an effective time advance
> through anomalous weak values, even though every individual motional branch undergoes ordinary
> time dilation. We extend this analysis to a weak gravitational field and show that an effective
> time advance can arise even for simple coherent states of the quantum clock motion. We further
> illustrate this effect using a hydrogen-like atom, revealing how it manifests itself in the
> atomic wavefunction. Our results demonstrate that quantum interference and postselection can
> produce an effective time advance beyond conventional relativistic time dilation.
