# Maximal Orbital Entanglement from Non-Interacting Fermions

## Abstract

> Orbital rotations are used in quantum many-body physics and chemistry to reduce the complexity
> of quantum states by compressing their correlation into a small set of relevant orbitals. In
> striking contrast, quantum information processing strives for the opposite: orbitals that
> maximize entanglement as a resource. Motivated by this, we prove that Slater determinants of $N$
> fermions on a lattice of $d_A + d_B = d$ sites, although free of particle correlation, can carry
> up to $\min\{d_A, d_B, N, d-N\}$ ebits of orbital entanglement. Given enough particles and
> holes, this equals the maximum over all fermionic states. For arbitrary free states, we derive
> the maximal orbital total correlation in closed form for all bipartitions, the finest partition,
> and all partitions of up to five sites, using new majorization relations for Hermitian matrices.
> Taken together, these results turn mean-field states, the starting point of most many-body
> methods in physics and chemistry, into a viable resource for quantum information processing.
