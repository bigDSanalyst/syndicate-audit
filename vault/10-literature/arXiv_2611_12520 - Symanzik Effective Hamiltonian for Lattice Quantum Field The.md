# Symanzik Effective Hamiltonian for Lattice Quantum Field Theories

## Abstract

> We propose a framework to construct the Symanzik effective Hamiltonian for lattice quantum field
> theories directly based on dimensional analysis and symmetries in the Hamiltonian formulation.
> This allows to classify all operators which can contribute at a given order in the spatial
> lattice spacing. This list can be reduced using the equations of motion and other identities. We
> exemplify the framework for $φ^4$ and pure gauge theory, obtaining results consistent with
> earlier publications for the case of classical improvement. It provides the means to develop a
> non-perturbative improvement programme in the Hamiltonian formulation of lattice quantum field
> theories in the future.
